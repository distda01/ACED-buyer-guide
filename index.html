The A.C.E.D. Buyer's Guide

A Framework for Connecting with Any Buyer

Based on the Playbook by Sales Gravy

Flex to Connect

You build deeper emotional connections when you interact with buyers based on who they are, not who you are.

  • The Similarity Bias: We naturally trust people who feel familiar.
  • Builds Instant Trust: Matching a buyer's style makes them more comfortable and open.
  • Reduces Friction: Eases buyer anxiety and opens the door for genuine connection.

The Four Buyer Personalities

🧩

Inclusion | Safety | Team

The Analyzer: "Just the facts."

Systematic, methodical, and logical. They need to see the data and will reject hype.

  • Core Need: To be correct.
  • Approach: Be over-prepared, back up claims with facts, and be patient.

Pro Tip: Win with methodical patience. Know your numbers cold.

The Director: "Get to the point."

Action-oriented, fast-moving, and decisive. They value salespeople who get things done quickly.

  • Core Need: To be in control.
  • Approach: Project confidence, prioritize business, and be concise.

Pro Tip: Respond to their challenges with respectful confidence.

The Energizer: "Let's connect!"

Relational, animated, and emotional. For them, the relationship comes before the business.

  • Core Need: To be appreciated and feel significant.
  • Approach: Listen intently, ask open-ended questions, and build rapport.

Pro Tip: They will often buy from you simply because they like you.

The Consensus Builder: "Let's get everyone on board."

Inclusive, risk-averse, and friendly. They want to ensure everyone feels included in the decision.

  • Core Need: To have security and be included.
  • Approach: Slow down, earn trust, and map out stakeholders.

Pro Tip: The fastest path to a decision is a group meeting with all stakeholders.

Winning Them Over

The Analyzer

✅ Present objective data

✅ Build the relationship slowly

❌ Don't get too personal

❌ Don't be unprepared

The Director

✅ Use bullet points & be concise

✅ Get right to the point

❌ Don't fake an answer

❌ Don't act weak or scared

Building the Relationship

The Energizer

✅ Let them talk & be the star

✅ Use an organized agenda

❌ Don't interrupt them

❌ Don't jump straight to business

The Consensus Builder

✅ Agree on a clear decision timeline

✅ Give short answers & ask questions

❌ Don't use high-pressure tactics

❌ Don't ignore their need for group buy-in

How to Adapt in Real-Time

🔎

vs. Analyzer: Slow down. Bring data.

👑

vs. Director: Speed up. Be confident.

⭐

vs. Energizer: Build rapport. Listen more.

🧩

vs. Consensus Builder: Facilitate. Connect the group.
